MAX98357A speaker and INMP441 microphone
=======================================

Shared I²S clocks (Pico 2 is the I²S master):
  GP10 -> MAX98357A BCLK and INMP441 SCK
  GP11 -> MAX98357A LRC/LRCLK/WS and INMP441 WS

MAX98357A (speaker):
  GP12 -> DIN
  VBUS -> VIN (USB 5 V)
  GND  -> GND
  Speaker between SPK+ and SPK-, neither terminal grounded.

  SD/EN is controlled by the breakout's pull resistors, not the firmware.
  Leaving it unconnected requires a breakout with an onboard enable pullup.
  If the tone is silent, check that SD/EN is not held low. GAIN can be left
  unconnected. No MCLK or I2C control connection is needed.

INMP441 (microphone):
  GP13 -> SD
  3V3  -> VDD and CHIPEN
  GND  -> GND and L/R (left channel)
  Do not connect L/R to 3V3 unless you want the right I²S slot instead.

Flash build/zephyr/zephyr.uf2 through BOOTSEL. Select "RP2350 UAC+CDC" as
the computer's audio output and input. USB is 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo playback
and 48 kHz, 16-bit mono record. Speaker playback averages left/right and
sends the same mono mix in both I2S slots. USB record is the INMP441 left
channel (24-bit samples truncated to 16-bit).

Open the CDC serial port at 115200 baud (see README.md for port selection):
  tone       Play a quiet 1 kHz tone for one second, without host playback.
  vol 25     Set speaker software volume, 0..100 percent (default 25).
  vol 0      Mute the speaker.
  noloop     USB record from the INMP441 (default).
  loop       Copy USB playback to the USB recording stream; ignore the mic.
  boot       Return to the BOOTSEL USB bootloader.

The tone temporarily replaces speaker playback. Volume applies to both
the tone and playback, but not USB recording.

Build:
  Follow README.md to activate your environment and select the Zephyr checkout.
  From the project directory:
  west build -b rpi_pico2/rp2350a/m33 .

The combined USB audio driver patch in patches/ must be applied to any
Zephyr checkout missing the fixes. See patches/README.txt for instructions.

Implementation:
  PIO0 SM0 generates 32-bit I2S: 48 kHz LRCLK, 3.072 MHz BCLK (64 clocks
  per frame) so the INMP441 and MAX98357A can share GP10/GP11.
  PIO0 SM1 waits on those clocks and captures the left 32-bit slot from GP13.
  Two chained DMA channels per direction supply 2 ms buffers. DMA IRQ0
  refills TX and harvests RX; no audio busy loop.
  The application owns DMA IRQ0 and initializes the DMA controller.
  CONFIG_DMA must stay disabled to avoid a second driver owning that IRQ.
  USB playback uses a locked 8 ms ring, a 4 ms prefill, silence on underrun,
  and occupancy-based asynchronous USB feedback to compensate clock drift.
  GP10/GP11 must stay adjacent; assignments are in app.overlay.

Host tests:
  python3 tests/test_audio.py

The build and host tests do not verify wiring or physical speaker/mic audio.
